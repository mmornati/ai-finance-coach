# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full, authoritative text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> (and in other languages at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations/>). It is incorporated here by reference: it applies to everyone taking part in the project's
spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to people representing the project in public.

In short (the covenant's text prevails):

* Be welcoming and respectful. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Focus on what is best for the community.
* Harassment, personal or political attacks, insults, discrimination, doxxing and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.
* Never publish other people's private information without consent. In this project that includes **financial data**: do not paste real
  statements, balances, names or account numbers into issues or pull requests, yours or anyone else's. Use invented data.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer, [@mmornati](https://github.com/mmornati) on GitHub (a private security advisory on the repository
reaches the maintainer alone: <https://github.com/mmornati/ai-finance-coach/security/advisories/new>). Reports are reviewed and answered promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant,
following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
